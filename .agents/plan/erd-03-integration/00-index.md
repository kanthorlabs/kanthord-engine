# Plan 00: Index

## Scope

ERD 3 ("External integration") covers the tables `intake_inbound`, `intake_inbound_event` and `intake_outbound_request` (`docs/reference/erd/03-integration.md:50–83`), together with the operations, collaborations, CLI commands and fixtures that make them usable end-to-end: the Intake Service module and its service identity; the outbound record with its repeat rules and its human operations; the configured repository actions `github.pull_request` and `git.merge_push`, the pull-request read and the check of a request evidence; the presigned storage grants and `s3.delete_object`; the inbound with its credential release; the webhook and the poll acquisition of GitHub; delivery admission; the handoff with the human event operations; the inbound resource healthcheck; and the OpenAPI and CLI of all of these.

ERD 3 replaces every ERD 2 seam that ERD 2 decision D6 left `unwired` (decision D6):

- `IntakeCheck.check`, `IntakeActions.perform`, `IntakeActions.read`: plan 03.
- `IntakeStorage.put`, `check`, `get`, `executionGet`, `delete`: plan 04.

The out-of-scope boundary is decision D5: the CLI transport and every CLI write, Slack, Telegram and Jira, the native push send, the inbound request contract (POSTPONED), acceptance as a human act and the account mapping, the cross-process identity contract (POSTPONED), the MCP server and its read tools, every HANDOFF failure and recovery item and the stored telemetry of ERD 4. A task that meets an open HANDOFF item implements the page as written and states the gap in one line (decision D1).

The decisions D1 to D24 that the plans cite are in [decisions.md](decisions.md).

**Rationale for order.**
The foundation (01) creates the tables, the service identity and the `service` policy that every later plan needs. The outbound record (02) is the write path of every outbound operation, so it precedes the repository actions (03) and the object delete (04). Plan 03 generalizes the protected facility, which plans 04 and 05 extend. The inbound store (05) precedes the webhook (06) and the poll (07), which insert events; the event reads sit in plan 06, so the first writer of `intake_inbound_event` proves its rows through a CLI read (ERD 1 decision D13). Delivery admission (08) needs the check of plan 03, and it precedes the handoff (09), which calls it (decision D5). Plan 10 assembles what crosses plans.

**Collaboration-type contract rule.**
ERD 2 `00-index.md` "Collaboration-type contract rule" stands: a service declares the types of its collaborations inline in its own `contract.ts`, and the composition root is the only file that wires cross-service types (`engine/src/apps/server/index.ts:74–319`; `architecture.impl.md:593–624`).
