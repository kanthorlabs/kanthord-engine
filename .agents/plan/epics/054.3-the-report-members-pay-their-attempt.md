# EPIC 054.3 — The report members pay their attempt

Status: **draft**. It follows EPIC 054.2 by sequence order, and it runs before EPIC 054.4. It consumes EPIC 054.1's `endAttempt`, EPIC 052.2's structural report member and EPIC 053.1's review report member.

It carries a decimal number because EPIC 055, EPIC 056 and EPIC 057 are already numbered, and renumbering them would move every cross-reference.

**Dispatch prerequisite.** EPIC 054.2 ships before this one. Story 1 inserts `"054.3"` into `scripts/epic-sequence-range.ts:1` — `authoredEpics` after `"054.2"`, and Story 5 appends it to `shippedEpics`, which `test/sequence/conformance.test.ts:254` — `shippedEpics` requires to stay a prefix of `authoredEpics`. Story 5 is therefore last in dispatch order.

**It holds seven stories: five changed paths, the checkpoint derivation and the proposal.** Three of the five are drawn here for the first time, because every diagram of `node.report` drawn so far covers an accepted arm. The other two are those accepted arms, which close their attempt through `end-attempt` from this epic on.

## Goal

A report that is not accepted charges the class the daemon observed:

- a worker's own failure report charges `worker-reported-failure`, which is semantic;
- a structural rejection and a review rejection each charge `daemon-rejected`, which is semantic;
- all three settle inside the prelude transaction `reportOutcome` already owns, and none opens a second;
- the failure report discards its candidate ref after that transaction commits, and the other two have none to discard;
- the accepted structural patch and the accepted review attestation close through the same command, so every close appends one event;
- every checkpoint writer records `caller` and `subject` from the same two sources as the attempt.

## Non-goals

- **No change to the report route's execution arm.** `report-lease-free` of EPIC 050.4 Story 6 (`06-the-report-drops-the-lease`) and `report-checkpoint-reap` of EPIC 051.6 Story 3 (`03-the-report-reaps-on-every-settled-terminal`) stay as drawn: `report-checkpoint-gate` already moved the execution close into the nested accept, and EPIC 054.2 owns that close. **The structural and the review accepted arms do change**, because each still closes its own attempt.
- **No termination on an accepted arm.** The two accepted stories change the caller of the close and nothing about the class: the row keeps a null `termination`, and the event records the acceptance with its `headOid`.
- **No refusal diagram of the two nested accepts moves.** `acceptStructural` and `acceptReview` take the caller's transaction and open none — `.agents/plan/epics/052.1-the-structural-acceptance.md:33` — `acceptStructural` — so the termination write is one statement after the accept returns, and the nine refusal diagrams of EPIC 052.1 and EPIC 053.1 keep their traces.
- **No execution gate arm.** EPIC 054.2 owns the five gate refusals and the contended land.
- **No backfill.** A checkpoint written before this epic keeps a null caller and a null subject, for the reason the attempt row keeps them.
- **No new transaction.** The prelude transaction is open on all three paths, so this epic adds no `storage.transact` step anywhere.

## Decisions

- **The three paths settle in `reportOutcome`'s prelude transaction.** `.agents/plan/epics/051.4-the-acceptance-gate-and-the-report-route.md:40` — `reportOutcome` states the prelude transaction commits before the git-bearing accept runs, and the failure member, the structural member and the review member all reach their verdict inside it. A second transaction is refused, because `AGENTS.md` names the two-unjournaled-transactions shape a defect it intends to repair.

- **A rejection is a returned disposition here too, and for the same reason as EPIC 054.2.** A throw inside `storage.transact` rolls back the settlement. `acceptStructural` and `acceptReview` return the rejection, `reportOutcome` writes the termination, commits, and then raises the refusal. `.agents/plan/epics/051.6-the-post-expiry-reap-on-the-run-operations.md:64` — `disposition` records that both shapes are legal.

- **These three paths are drawn for the first time, so each declares `Baselines:`.** `report-lease-free` draws the accepted arm — its `Seams:` line at `.agents/plan/stories/050.4-the-node-lease-removal/06-the-report-drops-the-lease.md:11` — `plan.setNodeState:T:outcome-accepted` holds the accepted node write — and every later report diagram supersedes an accepted arm. No epic has drawn a failure arm, so a `baseline-` diagram of shipped code is the prior set, and `.agents/plan/authoring.md:252` — `shipped code is drawn once` admits it, because no earlier epic drew that path.

- **A worker's own failure report is `worker-reported-failure`, and it is semantic.** The three worker-driven members `rejected`, `failed` and `cancelled` reach one arm at `src/commands/outcome/report-outcome.ts:234` — `closeAttempt`, and one diagram covers them, because their seam set and order are identical and only the stored outcome differs.

- **A worker's claim of quota exhaustion is not `provider-quota`.** Only a response the daemon's own transport received carries a `responseHash`, so a worker that reports "out of quota" still pays a semantic attempt. This is the rule EPIC 054 settled, and this epic is where a caller could have broken it.

- **The failure report discards its candidate ref, and the structural and review rejections have none.** An execution run may hold `refs/kanthord/candidate/<runId>/<attemptNo>` when the worker reports a failure, and the discard is this epic's, after the report transaction commits. A **structural** report carries a patch and no object id, and a **review** claim writes no candidate ref at all — `.agents/plan/epics/053.1-the-review-checkpoint.md:72` — `claim-success-review` states it — so those two arms call `candidate.discard` zero times, and the case asserts the count rather than assuming it.

- **The six conditions hold for the one discard.** The settlement commits before the deletion, so nothing rides with it; eligibility is the committed attempt close over an attempt-scoped ref; a failure leaves an inert ref; and `candidate.sweep` retries it at startup — `.agents/plan/stories/051.1-the-candidate-and-the-git-primitives/08-the-candidate-namespace-is-enumerated.md:143` — `findings`.

- **The reported run's own ref is `candidate.discard` and never `candidate.reap`.** `.agents/plan/epics/051.6-the-post-expiry-reap-on-the-run-operations.md:29` — `No reap inside` states both, and a reviewer who reads the reap as having moved the discard is reading two calls as one.

- **The two accepted arms close through `end-attempt`, and their diagrams move by one token.** `.agents/plan/stories/052.1-the-structural-acceptance/09-the-accepted-patch.md:14` — `accept-structural-success` and `.agents/plan/stories/053.1-the-review-checkpoint/10-the-report-route-carries-a-verdict.md:14` — `report-review-gate` each hold `execution.closeAttempt:A`, and each becomes `endAttempt:A`. No other step of either diagram moves, and neither arm gains a transaction: `endAttempt` takes the one its caller already holds. EPIC 054 rules why an acceptance appends the event, and EPIC 054.1 rules that an accepted ending shares the seam set of a semantic one.

- **Every checkpoint writer records `caller` and `subject`, and this epic is their first filler.** `.agents/plan/epics/051.3-the-checkpoint-and-the-land.md:29` — `caller` created both columns nullable and named EPIC 054's family as their filler. `WriteCheckpointInput` gains both, and each of the three writers — the accepted land, the structural acceptance and the review attestation — passes `subject` from `run.worker` and `caller` from the authenticated principal. **This changes no drawn path**: `execution.writeCheckpoint` keeps its token and its projection, so no diagram of EPIC 051.3, EPIC 052.1 or EPIC 053.1 moves.

## Stories

Each entry is a name and the output it contributes. The story file holds the change, the tasks and the diagrams, and it declares its kind. `.agents/plan/authoring.md` is the standard.

1. **The worker's own failure member pays its attempt.** Draw `baseline-report-worker-failure` and `report-worker-failure-paid`: the prelude, the accounting read, `endAttempt` in place of the direct close, the node write, and `candidate.discard` after the transaction commits. 3. **A review rejection pays its attempt.** The same for `acceptReview`, over the six judged refusals of EPIC 053.1. Draw `baseline-report-review-rejection` and `report-review-rejection`. `story-implement`.

4. **The accepted structural patch closes through the command.** Replace the direct close of `acceptStructural` with `endAttempt` carrying the accepted member, and draw `accept-structural-success-paid`, superseding EPIC 052.1 Story 9 (`09-the-accepted-patch`) `accept-structural-success`. `story-implement`.

5. **The accepted review attestation closes through the command.** The same for the review member, and draw `report-review-gate-paid`, superseding EPIC 053.1 Story 10 (`10-the-report-route-carries-a-verdict`) `report-review-gate`. The attestation closes with a null `headOid`, so the accepted member carries null there. `story-implement`.

6. **The checkpoint pair is derived.** Extend `WriteCheckpointInput` with `caller` and `subject`, and pass both at the accepted land, the structural acceptance and the review attestation. `story-foundation`.

7. **The proposal records the report arms.** Amend `docs/proposal/phase-2/attempts-and-classification.md` with the three rejection arms, the two accepted arms, the returned-disposition rule, the discard ownership per arm and the checkpoint derivation. Append `"054.3"` to `shippedEpics`. It is last in dispatch order. `story-foundation`.
